What is this?

A booking channel is the route a reservation comes through: an online travel agent, a tour operator, a group block, a corporate account, or the hotel's own website and phone line. This scorecard compares the channels side by side and gives each one a verdict.

Push = under 20% cancel, so grow it. Monitor = 20–40%, so keep it with reminders. Restrict = 40% or more, so ask for deposits or limit the rooms it gets.

In one line: This tells marketing which channels to push and which to restrict.

What is the threshold?

The model gives every booking a cancellation probability between 0 and 1. The threshold is the cut-off: any booking at or above it is flagged as "will cancel".

Example: at 0.50, a booking with 62% risk is flagged; one with 40% is not.

  • Lower threshold: catches more cancellations, but more loyal guests are flagged by mistake.
  • Higher threshold: fewer false alarms, but more cancellations are missed.

In one line: The threshold is the risk level at which the hotel decides to act.
